# 4K Download

Official GitHub account of [4K Download](https://www.4kdownload.com): desktop apps for saving and converting video, audio and images on Windows, macOS and Linux. Published by InterPromo GMBH, Baden-Baden, Germany.

We use this account to publish our apps to the [Windows Package Manager (winget)](https://github.com/microsoft/winget-pkgs).

## Apps

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| [4K Video Downloader Plus](https://www.4kdownload.com/products/videodownloader) | Download video and audio from YouTube and similar services |
| [4K YouTube to MP3](https://www.4kdownload.com/products/youtubetomp3) | Convert YouTube videos and playlists to MP3 |
| [4K Tokkit](https://www.4kdownload.com/products/tokkit) | Download TikTok videos, hashtags and accounts |
| [4K Image Compressor](https://www.4kdownload.com/products/imagecompressor) | Compress and resize images |

Also available: [4K Video to MP3](https://www.4kdownload.com/products/videotomp3), [4K Slideshow Maker](https://www.4kdownload.com/products/slideshowmaker).
